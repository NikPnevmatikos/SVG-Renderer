# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of experience level,
background, identity or the tools they use. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free
experience and to treat each other with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and ask before judging.
- Respect differing viewpoints and the time of volunteers.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination in any form.
- Insults, personal attacks or demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public
channel where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to `nikpnevmatikos.oss@gmail.com`. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments
that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour
they deem inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
